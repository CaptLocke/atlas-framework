# Nova

**Nova extends what you can hold in your head at work.** It remembers what was said and decided, keeps track of who owes what, prepares you for what's next, drafts your routine writing, and quietly does the upkeep you never get to, all inside Claude.

Nova is for individual knowledge workers: operators, product and project managers, sales, support, finance, and anyone whose job is decisions, communication, and coordination. You don't need to be technical.

## What Nova does

- **Remembers for you.** People, projects, decisions, commitments, ideas, and where each fact came from, all connected.
- **Finds what matters automatically.** Ask a question or start a task; Nova brings the relevant knowledge with it and cites its sources.
- **Works while you're away.** Every evening (or more often, depending on your Claude plan) Nova brings in new email, meetings, and messages, triages your commitments, drafts replies, prepares tomorrow's meetings, and writes your morning brief.
- **Keeps you oriented.** A daily brief, a weekly review, and a set of always-current dashboards.
- **Sounds like you.** Drafts follow your own voice and your company's style and branding.
- **Stays under your control.** You decide how much Nova does on its own. Nova never sends, shares, or deletes anything without your approval, and logs everything it does.

## What you need

- A **Claude Pro** plan or higher, using the Claude desktop app (macOS or Windows).
- A free **GitHub** account. Nova keeps your knowledge in a private repository that only you can see.

That's it: no other subscriptions, servers, or software.

## Get started

Follow the **[setup guide](docs/setup/README.md)**. It walks you through three one-time steps, then Nova takes over in conversation. Most people finish in under an hour, and you can stop and resume at any point.

## Your data

Everything Nova learns about your work lives in **your own private repository**, in plain, readable files. You can read it, export it, or take it elsewhere at any time. Before Nova stores anything, it asks you to confirm you're permitted to keep work information there.

## Updates

Nova updates itself. New versions replace only Nova's own files, never your knowledge, profile, or customizations. See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
