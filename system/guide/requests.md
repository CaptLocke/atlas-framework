# Things you can say

A cheat sheet. Use your own words: Nova understands the meaning, not exact phrases. Names here are just examples.

## Remember
Tell Nova anything worth keeping, in one sentence.
- "Remember that the auditor prefers PDFs."
- "I told Jordan I'd send the roadmap by Tuesday."
- "Priya promised the signed scope by the 21st."
- "We decided to start the pilot with the EMEA team because NA is mid-migration."
- "Idea: pilot fees count toward year one."
- "Morning thoughts: …" / "Evening thoughts: …"
- "Here's the contract." (attach the file)
- "Here's how we run month-end close: …" (Nova saves it as a playbook)

## Find
Ask about anything in your work. Nova shows where each answer came from.
- "What did we decide about pricing, and why?"
- "What do I owe people this week?"
- "What am I waiting on from Dana?"
- "When did I last speak to Priya, and what's open between us?"
- "What do we know about churn?"
- "Where was I on the vendor evaluation?"
- "Catch me up on Globex."
- "What happened while I was away?"
- To browse everything Nova has filed, open the **Records** page and search by name, type, or status.

## Prepare
Get ready for what's next.
- "What's on today?"
- "Prep me for my 2pm."
- "Prep me for my 1:1 with Jordan."
- "What should I know before the Globex call?"
- "What's coming up next week?"
- "Give me a briefing on the enterprise launch."

## Write
Drafts and finished work, in your voice and your company's style.
- "Draft a reply to Priya."
- "Write my status update for Jordan."
- "Follow up with Sam on the SSO estimate."
- "Turn these notes into a one-pager."
- "Make a five-slide deck on the pilot results."
- "Build a spreadsheet comparing the three vendors."
- "Make this shorter." / "Make this sound more like me."

## Decide
Think things through with your own knowledge behind you.
- "What are the options for the liability clause, and what has Dana said before?"
- "What would slip if I move the launch a week?"
- "Which of my commitments matter most for my Q4 goals?"
- "What am I missing on the Globex pilot?"
- "Argue the other side."

## Review
Look back and look ahead.
- "How did this week go?"
- "What did I get done this quarter?"
- "How am I doing on my goals?"
- "Rank my goals." / "Hiring matters more than the launch now."
- "What have you learned about how I work?"
- "Which commitments slipped this month?"
- "Who haven't I talked to in a while?"
- "Put together my performance review notes for Q4."

## Control
Change how Nova behaves, any time.
- "What did you do overnight?"
- "Undo what you did to the Globex record this morning."
- "Forget that." / "Permanently erase everything about that candidate."
- "Don't track anything from the #random channel."
- "Ask me before updating anything about Globex."
- "Stop suggesting ideas in the brief."
- "I'm out from the 12th to the 20th."
- "Pause the weekly review until November."
- "Move my brief to be ready by 6:30."
- "Switch me to the Standard automation profile."
- "What do you know about me?"
- "Update Nova." / "What's new?"
- "Is everything working?"
- "Report an issue: …"
- "Start Nova" (if Nova ever seems not to know your setup)
