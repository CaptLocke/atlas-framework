# What Nova does

Nova is your personal operating layer for work. It remembers so you don't have to, keeps your work moving while you're away, points out what deserves attention, and helps you produce better work faster. Everything below works by simply asking in your Nova chat. The names in the examples are just examples.

## Connected memory
Nova keeps an organized, connected memory of your work: people, organizations, projects, goals, commitments, decisions, meetings, risks, questions, ideas, topics, and how-tos. Everything is linked to what it relates to and to where it came from. Nothing is thrown away; when something changes, Nova keeps the history.

**Try:**
- "What do we know about Globex?"
- "Who's involved in the enterprise launch?"
- "Show me the history of the pricing decision."

## Relevant recall
You never have to say "check my notes." Whenever you ask something or start a task, Nova finds what's relevant (the person, their open requests, the related decision, the earlier meeting) and tells you where each fact came from. If it doesn't know, it says so.

**Try:**
- "What did we decide about the enterprise tier last quarter, and why?"
- "What did Priya ask for in our last call?"
- "When did I last talk to Dana?"

## Picking up where you left off
Nova keeps the thread of your work across days and chats. Starting a new chat never means starting over, and coming back from time off means a short catch-up, not an inbox mountain.

**Try:**
- "Where was I on the vendor evaluation?"
- "Catch me up on the Globex pilot."
- "What changed while I was in meetings today?"

## Bringing information in
Nova reads your connected email, calendar, meeting transcripts, chat, and files, and files what matters. It ignores newsletters, notifications, and automated messages. You can also tell it things in one sentence, attach a file in chat, or drop a file into your **Nova Drop** folder.
- Clear promises ("I'll send it Friday", "Dana will review by Tuesday") are recorded as commitments automatically.
- Vague ones ("we should look into that") become questions, or Nova asks you to confirm them in your brief.

**Try:**
- "Remember that the auditor prefers PDFs."
- "Here's the signed pilot scope." (with the file attached)
- "File this meeting: we agreed to start the pilot with the EMEA team."

## Morning and Evening Thoughts
Twice a day, if you like, brain-dump whatever's on your mind, typed or dictated, as messy as you want. Nova keeps your words exactly as you said them in your journal, then pulls out commitments, ideas, questions, and decisions and links them to the right places.

**Try:**
- "Morning thoughts: …"
- "Evening thoughts: …"
- "What did I brain-dump about hiring this month?"

## Help doing the work
Nova writes, analyzes, prepares, and plans with you, grounded in what it knows and in your voice. It picks the right format (a message, a document, a deck, a spreadsheet) and produces finished work, not rough notes. Finished files are saved to your deliverables folder and offered as a download.

**Try:**
- "Prep me for the board meeting."
- "Turn this into a one-pager."
- "Draft a reply to Priya about the security review."

## Playbooks
When you do the same kind of work again and again (renewals, onboarding a new hire, month-end close), Nova notices and offers to save it as a playbook. Next time one starts, Nova sets up the checklist with dates, has the right messages drafted when each step comes due, and tells you if a step is missed or out of order. Insights that suggest messaging someone come with the draft already written too.

**Try:**
- "Save how we do renewals as a playbook."
- "Start the renewal playbook for Brightline."
- "Add a step to the onboarding playbook: send the welcome pack."

## Your Daily Brief
Every morning, a short brief answers: what's on today, what to prepare, what's due or slipping, what changed overnight, what you're waiting on, and your top priorities. It includes up to three insights, drafts ready for review, and anything waiting for your approval. It's built to read in two minutes.

**Try:**
- "What's on today?"
- "Show me my brief."
- "What's slipping?"

## Insights
Nova notices things: commitments at risk, decisions without owners, unanswered questions, people who've gone quiet, the same issue appearing in different places. Each insight says why it matters and where it came from. You'll see up to three in your brief, and one short insight with a suggested next step on every open commitment and project.

**Try:**
- "What should I be worried about this week?"
- "What patterns do you see in customer feedback this month?"
- "Turn that insight into a risk."

## Automations
Routine work happens on its own, in the cloud, even when your computer is off: bringing in new information, triaging commitments, drafting replies and follow-ups, preparing for meetings, writing your brief, weekly reviews and status updates, and monthly and quarterly check-ins. You agreed once; it happens every time. See **Automations** for what runs when.

**Try:**
- "What runs automatically?"
- "Every Monday, summarize last week's pipeline changes from the CRM."
- "Move my weekly review to Thursday afternoon."

## Made for you
Everything Nova does is shaped by what it knows about you: your role, company, goals, working style, and how you like to receive information. When Nova notices something new about how you work, it asks before treating it as fact. You can see and change everything it believes about you on the **About You** dashboard.

Nova also learns from your corrections. When you keep dropping the same kind of "commitment", raising the same kind of work, or dismissing the same kind of insight, it suggests a rule ("Stop treating 'I'll take a look' as a promise?") and follows it once you say yes. The monthly report shows what it learned.

**Try:**
- "What do you know about how I like to work?"
- "I prefer numbers first in briefs."
- "That's wrong: my manager is Jordan, not Sam."
- "That's not a commitment." / "That insight wasn't useful."

## Your voice and your company's style
Nova keeps a personal voice guide (how you write), a house voice guide (how your company writes), and a design system (your company's colors, fonts, and layouts). Customer-facing and official material uses the house voice; everything else uses yours. Drafts aim to pass the "would I send this as-is?" test, and Nova learns from your edits.

**Try:**
- "Make this sound more like me."
- "Write this in our company voice for customers."
- "Use our brand colors for this deck."

## Memory controls
You decide what Nova remembers. Correct anything, tell it to stop tracking a source, or have it forget something. See **Controls** for the difference between "forget" and "permanently erase".

**Try:**
- "Forget that."
- "Don't track anything from the #random channel."
- "Correct that: the launch moved to November 9."

## Seeing and controlling automations
The **Control Center** dashboard shows every automation, when it last ran, when it runs next, what it did, and anything waiting for your approval. Problems come with a plain fix.

**Try:**
- "What did you do overnight?"
- "Pause the weekly review until November."
- "Why didn't my brief include today's meetings?"

## Guided setup
Setup is a conversation. Nova interviews you, learns from your examples, connects your tools, and sets up your automations and dashboards. Every question can be skipped, and you can stop and resume any time.

**Try:**
- "Continue setting up Nova."
- "Connect my calendar."
- "Change my automation profile."

## Quiet, reliable running
Nova handles saving and syncing itself. You'll never be asked to save, sync, or manage files. If a connection breaks, Nova keeps working with what it has and tells you what's missing and how to fix it.

**Try:**
- "Is everything working?"
- "Which connections need attention?"

## Self-upkeep
Once a month Nova tidies up: merging duplicates, fixing broken links, flagging stale items, and checking its own automations. It only brings you real decisions.

**Try:**
- "Run a health check."
- "Are there any duplicate people in my Nova?"

## Updates
New versions of Nova arrive on their own. Your knowledge, profile, and customizations are never touched. Small updates apply automatically; bigger ones wait for your yes. You'll see what's new in this Guide and in your next brief, and you can always go back to the previous version.

**Try:**
- "What's new in Nova?"
- "Update Nova."
- "Go back to the previous version."

## Make it your own
Add your own automations, alerts, record types (such as Deals or Cases), how-to playbooks, and dashboards, just by describing them. They survive every update.

**Try:**
- "Create an automation that preps me for every sales call with the account's history."
- "Add a Deals record type with stage and value."
- "When a deal moves to negotiation, build me a deal brief."

## Away mode
Going on holiday or heads-down for a week? Tell Nova your dates. It keeps filing everything, pauses your brief, drafts, status updates, and reminders, and has a catch-up brief waiting when you're back. It doesn't write an out-of-office reply.

**Try:**
- "I'm out from the 12th to the 20th."
- "I'm back early."
- "What did I miss?"

## Reporting a problem
Tell Nova something's wrong in one sentence. It gathers the details Nova's maker needs, removes anything personal or sensitive, and drafts an email for you to review and send.

**Try:**
- "Report an issue: my brief didn't include today's meetings."
- "Something's broken with my calendar sync. Can you report it?"

## Dashboards
Always-current pages you can open any time: **Daily Brief**, **Work Board** (every project and commitment), **Goals** (with your running list of accomplishments), **Week Ahead**, **People**, **Decisions & Risks**, **Control Center**, **About You**, **Records** (search everything Nova has filed, by name, type, or status), and this **Nova Guide**. Ask for a page for any project.

**Try:**
- "Open my Work Board."
- "Make a project page for the enterprise launch."
- "Add a dashboard of all open customer requests."
