## Person
Sections: Who they are · History (5–8 dated lines) · Open items both ways · What they care about · How they like to communicate · Next touchpoint · Talking points.

## Topic / deal
Sections: Where it stands (3 lines) · How we got here (decisions, dated) · People and organizations · Numbers · Risks and open questions · What's next · Suggested position.
